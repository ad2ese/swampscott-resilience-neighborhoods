Planning Read

Olmsted/Shaw Estate/Redington is the clearest self-defined cluster in this sample. Several other areas appear as one- to three-response signals, which means the absence of mentions should be treated as an outreach gap, not evidence that residents there lack interest.

Administrative and EJ Context

Precinct boundaries are included because some residents described neighborhood scale in administrative terms. Swampscott has 6 2022 voting precincts in this layer.

The optional MA Environmental Justice overlay shows 41 nearby 2020 Census block groups that meet one or more state EJ criteria, including 2 within Swampscott. Use it as an equity-screening reference alongside, not in place of, resident-defined neighborhood signals.

Sources and Methods

Survey export: Swampscott Community Survey.csv, 39 rows, response window 2026-07-22 to 2026-07-29. Public geography references: MassGIS municipal boundaries, MassGIS 2022 Wards and Precincts, MassGIS 2020 Environmental Justice Populations, OpenStreetMap, Massachusetts MVP Planning 2.0, Swampscott MVP 2.0, Swampscott beaches, and Swampscott Olmsted Historic District.

Generated 2026-07-29. Neighborhood areas are approximate ellipses centered on public landmarks or roads mentioned in the survey; they deliberately do not attempt to infer respondent addresses. Precinct and EJ polygons are public contextual layers and should not be read as recommended resilience-hub boundaries.
